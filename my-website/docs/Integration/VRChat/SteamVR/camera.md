# Stream Camera

By default, the VRChat window shows a first-person view that moves with your head. With VRChat's Stream Camera, you can switch to a fixed third-person view for streaming or recording.

## Opening the Stream Camera

1. Open **Display VR View** in SteamVR. After you switch to Stream mode, the VRChat window no longer shows the first-person view, so you will need VR View to see what you are doing.
2. In Bridge, click **Mouse & Keyboard**, then press Q to open the Quick Menu.
3. Open the camera and switch its mode to **Stream**.

The VRChat window now shows the camera view, and you can continue operating in VR View.

## Flying Mode

With Flying mode on, the camera can leave your avatar and move freely, which is useful for third-person shots.

1. In VR View, click **Flying** in the camera settings.

   <video controls width="100%">
     <source src="/vrchat-stream-camera-flying-on.mp4"/>
   </video>

2. Press Alt+Tab to switch to the VRChat window, then use the mouse and keyboard to move the camera into position.

   <video controls width="100%">
     <source src="/vrchat-stream-camera-flying-move.mp4"/>
   </video>

3. Press Alt+Tab to switch to Bridge, turn off Flying mode, then exit Mouse & Keyboard control. The VRChat window now shows a fixed view that no longer follows your head.

   <video controls width="100%">
     <source src="/vrchat-stream-camera-flying-off.mp4"/>
   </video>

:::tip
Turn off Flying mode before you exit Mouse & Keyboard control. If you do it the other way around, the Stream Camera may flip upside down as your avatar's hands lower. To fix this, enter Mouse & Keyboard control again and press Q to open the camera settings, and the view will return to normal.

<video controls width="100%">
  <source src="/vrchat-stream-camera-flipped.mp4"/>
</video>
:::
